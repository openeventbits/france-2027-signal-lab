# France 2027 Signal Lab — Third-Party Notices

France 2027 Signal Lab (FR27) uses and references material originating from external sources.

This file identifies important third-party-material boundaries and explains how those materials relate to the FR27 licensing structure.

It is not a claim that FR27 owns third-party material.

It is not a substitute for the licence, terms, trademark rules, or other rights applicable to a particular external source.

## Licensing boundary

FR27's public licences apply only to material that the relevant FR27 licensor has authority to license.

The repository uses:

- [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) for covered FR27-created software;
- [`CONTENT_LICENSE.md`](CONTENT_LICENSE.md) for covered original FR27 non-software material; and
- [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) for required software attribution and licensing-boundary information.

Third-party material is excluded from those grants unless an express statement says otherwise.

The fact that third-party material appears:

- in a screenshot; or

does not transfer ownership of that material to FR27 and does not cause it to become licensed under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 or CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Third-party status categories

For purposes of repository maintenance, third-party material should be treated conservatively.

A material item can fall into one of these practical categories:

### Explicitly licensed

The applicable third-party licence or permission is known and documented.

That third-party licence or permission controls the relevant material.

### Identification material

The material is used to identify an external source, publisher, pollster, institution, service, or other entity.

Its inclusion does not mean FR27 owns the mark or grants downstream rights in it.

### Source-linked material

The material originates from an external evidence source and is retained or referenced for provenance, verification, reporting, or research context.

Applicable third-party rights remain with the relevant rights holder.

### Rights status not individually determined

The material has not been established to be FR27-owned or covered by an express third-party licence.

Such material must not be treated as freely reusable merely because it is publicly accessible or present in the repository.

An unknown or unrecorded rights status does not mean public domain.

## Source-icon provenance and review

The presence of an asset in `assets/source-icons/` is not itself a representation that the asset has been individually licensed by its rights holder.

FR27 treats this directory as a third-party identification-asset boundary.

Source-icon provenance and rights basis should be reviewed separately from the software and content licensing architecture.

Where a source-specific licence, permission, attribution requirement, or other relevant rights basis is established, it can be recorded in this file or in associated provenance records.

Where the status of a source-identification asset remains uncertain, that uncertainty does not convert the asset into FR27-created content.

Nothing in this section asserts that a particular copyright limitation, trademark rule, nominative-use doctrine, or other legal exception necessarily applies to every asset.

## Maintenance rule

When new third-party material is introduced, do not silently treat it as FR27-owned content.

Determine which of the following applies:

- an express third-party licence;
- documented permission;
- source-identification use;
- source-linked evidence;
- material whose rights status requires further review; or
- material that should not be incorporated.

Where an express third-party licence requires attribution or preservation of a notice, record that requirement.

Do not place third-party material under the FR27 PolyForm or CC BY-NC licence unless FR27 actually has authority to do so.
