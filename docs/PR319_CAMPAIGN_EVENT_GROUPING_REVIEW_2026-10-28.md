## Method and exact effects

The generic matcher has no candidate name/ID or article-URL allowlist. A validated occurrence registry supplies reviewed actor/action/place/date facts and evidence links. Every broader launch-group member must independently match exactly one occurrence, a single full named actor and explicit located place. It rejects compound actions, roundups, candidate-association ambiguity, conflicting dates/weekdays/places, previews and reports outside the 48-hour reporting bound. Unknown or ambiguous signatures do not fall back to title similarity. Located “se lance dans la course” can describe launch coverage; unlocated candidacy-declaration wording keeps the prior candidacy rules. Launch clusters require pairwise compatibility with every member, preventing transitive bridges. Existing non-launch support/alliance/complaint/candidacy rules remain unchanged.
